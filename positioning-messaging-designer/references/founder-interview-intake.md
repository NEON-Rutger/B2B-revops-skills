# Founder Interview Intake

Run this interview BEFORE any positioning canvas work. The output is raw material for your reframing; unwritten observations often waterlog the positioning downstream. Capture responses verbatim. Do not rephrase into cleaner marketing language.

## Interview Structure

Work through one pain point at a time. For each pain, ask the three triads below and wait for complete answers. Push for depth — the specific thing, with examples, not a headline.

### Triad 1: The Brokenness

**What is broken about your industry right now?**

Listen for a specific moment, a workflow, a repeated friction. Capture their exact language. Examples of depth:
- NOT "forecasting is hard" but "we rebuild the forecast in a spreadsheet three days before board, then sales argues it's too conservative, then we rebuild it again"
- NOT "data quality" but "when we run the monthly churn report, accounts show different closed dates in the pipeline than in billing, so we spend an hour reconciling before we know the real number"

**Why do you think it is broken?**

Get their diagnosis of root cause. This is their worldview of the problem, not a neutral statement. Capture it exactly.

Examples:
- "Because nobody built the right tool" (underinvestment in solutions)
- "Because founders are scared of the number so they avoid looking at it" (behaviour problem)
- "Because the system prioritised features over operations" (org misalignment)

**What do you think the solution is?**

Not "we need a platform." Get the MECHANISM. How would they fix it if they could?

Examples:
- "We'd measure it live, in one place, so nobody can argue about the number"
- "We'd automate the reconciliation, so the report IS the truth the moment we run it"
- "We would build an operating rhythm where the revenue team meets weekly and the forecast is a living thing, not a quarterly event"

### Repeat for Every Pain Point

Work through 3-5 pain points using the same structure. One triad (brokenness, why, fix) per pain.

---

## Diagnosis Section (Lightning Round)

### Interchangeability Check

After the brokenness interviews, ask:

**How do you currently describe what is broken in your market?**

Capture the exact words they use. Then ask:

**How many other consultants or companies use similar language to describe the same problem?**

The goal: identify shared language everyone uses (banned from final positioning) vs. distinctive language (theirs, keep it).

### Category Language Candidates

From the founder's own responses, extract:
- Phrases they repeat (they own these words)
- Problem names they use (not your names, theirs)
- Mechanisms they described (the how, not just the what)

List 5-8 candidate phrases directly from their answers to use in later reframing.

---

## What Good Looks Like

- Every answer is verbatim founder language, not your summary
- The three questions per pain point force them to diagnose, not describe
- You can read the interview transcript and hear their voice, not marketing copy
- At the end, you have 3-5 pain triads and a bank of their actual phrasing to draw from
- No positioning yet; just raw material

The mediocre version skips the interview, invents pain from the website, or paraphrases the founder's answers into cleaner language before doing the positioning work.
