# Sales Cycle Benchmarks by Segment

Last verified: 2026-09. Figures older than 2025 are kept only where no newer edition exists and are marked as such.

On-demand reference for the deal-velocity-engineer skill.

Always diagnose against segment-appropriate benchmarks. A 120-day enterprise cycle isn't slow; a 120-day SMB cycle is catastrophic.

| Segment | ACV Range | Benchmark Cycle | Optimal Range | Red Flag |
|---------|-----------|----------------|---------------|----------|
| SMB | <$15K | 14 to 30 days | 20 to 40 days | >60 days |
| Mid-Market | $15-75K | 60 to 90 days | 45 to 75 days | >120 days |
| Enterprise | $75-250K | 90 to 150 days | 90 to 120 days | >180 days |
| Strategic | >$250K | 120 to 180+ days | Depends on complexity | >270 days |

**Sources and evidence type:**
- Benchmark Cycle column: practice-based composite, consistent with the one vendor dataset located with segment bands (Optifai, 939 SaaS companies, vendor platform data: under $15K ACV 14 to 30 days, $15K to $100K 30 to 90 days, over $100K 90 to 180+ days, 84-day median). No surveyed primary source with these exact ACV bands was found.
- Optimal Range and Red Flag columns: practice-based operating thresholds, not statistics.
- Gong Labs (2024): 69-day median at EUR97K ACV (vendor platform data; older than 2025, not re-verified in the 2026-09 pass).
- Ebsta x Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks (655,000 opportunities, $48B pipeline; vendor platform data plus survey): top teams close deals 3x faster. https://www.joinpavilion.com/resource/2025-gtm-benchmarks-ebsta-pavilion
- Fullcast 2026 Revenue Benchmark Report ($78B pipeline, 361,000 opportunities, 2,500 reps; vendor platform data; Fullcast acquired Ebsta in August 2025). https://www.fullcast.com/content/fullcast-releases-2026-revenue-benchmark-report-analyzing-78-billion-in-revenue-data/

**Trend context (critical for your sales conversations):**
- Sales cycles ran **6.9% longer** year on year, one of three drivers behind a 28% fall in sales efficiency (deal value down 11.1%, win rate down 13.5%) (Fullcast/Pavilion 2026 GTM benchmarks, vendor platform data, as reported by SaaSletter: https://www.saasletter.com/p/ai-nrr-gross-margin-efficient-frontier-2026-gtm-sales-benchmarks-fullcast-pavilion)
- Lost deals consume **2.0x more selling days** than won deals (Fullcast 2026 Benchmarks Report, vendor platform data). https://www.fullcast.com/ebooks/2026-benchmarks-report-the-state-of-gtm-in-2026/
- Buying committees now include **10 to 15+ stakeholders** in a typical B2B deal (Fullcast, 2026, vendor content citing its 2026 benchmark report). https://www.fullcast.com/content/account-based-growth/
- Security and procurement reviews commonly add weeks to enterprise cycles (practice-based; measure your own security-review duration rather than relying on an external figure)

When your sales cycles are getting longer, that's not unusual. But the question is whether yours are longer than the market shift justifies.
