# Time-to-Value: The Retention Foundation

On-demand reference for the expansion-revenue-architect skill.

Last verified: 2026-09. Figures older than 2025 are kept only where no newer edition exists and are marked as such.

TTV is the most underrated lever for both GRR and expansion readiness. Customers who find value quickly retain better AND expand more.

## TTV-to-Retention Correlation (Sourced)

| TTV Achievement | Impact | Source |
|----------------|--------|--------|
| 7-day activation benchmark | **69% correlation** with 3-month retention; represents top-quartile activation performance | Amplitude 2025 (vendor platform data; not re-verified in the 2026-09 refresh) |
| No value milestone within 14 days | **98% churn within two weeks** | Amplitude 2025, 2,600+ B2B SaaS companies (vendor platform data; not re-verified in the 2026-09 refresh; check the original report wording before quoting externally) |

Removed in the 2026-09 refresh, because the only cited sources were aggregator blogs ("SaaS Onboarding Statistics 2026", Digital Applied 2026) that do not name a primary study:

- 80%+ month-12 retention for value within 14 days vs 35-50% beyond 30 days.
- 90% churn probability with no engagement in the first 3 days.
- 40% higher retention and 52% better Day-30 retention from personalized onboarding.
- Median SaaS TTV of 1 day 12 hours.
- 60-70% of annual churn in the first 90 days; 40-60% of users churning in the first 30 days.

## TTV Benchmarks by Segment

Practice-based targets (operator experience; no external study):

| Segment | Good | Great | Best-in-Class | Source |
|---------|------|-------|---------------|--------|
| SMB | <14 days | <7 days | <3 days | Practice-based |
| Mid-Market | <60 days | <30 days | <14 days | Practice-based |
| Enterprise | <90 days | <60 days | <30 days | Practice-based |

**Implication for expansion:** Fix TTV first, then build expansion. Customers who haven't reached value will never expand, and the first-90-days onboarding window is the make-or-break moment for the entire right side of the bowtie (practice-based; the prior "43% of SMB customer losses occur in the first 90 days, Chameleon 2024" figure is pre-2025 vendor data that could not be re-verified and has been removed).
