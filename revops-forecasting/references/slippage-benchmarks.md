# Slippage Benchmarks (Ebsta/Pavilion 2025)

Last verified: 2026-09. Figures older than 2025 are kept only where no newer edition exists and are marked as such.

On-demand reference for the revops-forecasting skill.

Sources: Ebsta x Pavilion 2025 GTM Benchmarks, reporting 2024 pipeline data (vendor platform data plus survey; 655,000 opportunities, $48B), https://www.joinpavilion.com/resource/2025-gtm-benchmarks-ebsta-pavilion and https://www.ebsta.com/exclusive/why-deal-slippage-growth-killer ; Ebsta 2025 Sales Qualification Report (vendor platform data), https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/new-ebsta-report-reveals-top-sales-teams-win-6-3x-more-often-by-qualifying-smarter-not-harder-302520694.html . No 2026 edition with a new slip rate was located; the 2026 Fullcast benchmark (Fullcast acquired Ebsta in August 2025) did not publish a replacement figure in the material reviewed.

```
MARKET SLIPPAGE RATES (vendor platform data):
  36% of pipeline deals slip (improved from 44% the prior year) [Ebsta x Pavilion 2025]
  Low performers are 217% more likely to experience deal slippage [Ebsta x Pavilion 2025]
  Strongly qualified deals are 1.9x less likely to slip past forecast close date [Ebsta Qualification 2025]
  Only 36% of deals passing Discovery carry both a qualification score and notes [Ebsta Qualification 2025]

SLIPPAGE DIAGNOSTIC (practice-based thresholds):
  If slippage >40%: Process problem; stage exit criteria not enforced
  If slippage 30-40%: Normal range; focus on the deals that slip repeatedly
  If slippage <25%: Strong discipline; maintain and coach to sustain

WHAT PREDICTS SLIPPAGE (practice-based heuristics; no primary source located for multipliers):
  Weak or missing qualification → 1.9x more likely to slip [Ebsta Qualification 2025]
  No critical event identified → elevated slip risk
  Single-threaded (one contact) → elevated slip risk
  No activity in 14+ days → deal is already dead, just not marked yet
  Close date unchanged for 3+ weeks → likely to slip

FORECASTING ADJUSTMENT (practice-based formula):
  Apply slippage haircut to Best Case and Upside categories:
  Best Case adjusted = Best Case × (1 - your historical slip rate)
  If 36% of Best Case deals slip, multiply Best Case by 0.64 for realistic forecast
```
